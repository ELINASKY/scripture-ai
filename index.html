NDI

Full NDI (sending and receiving every NDI source on the network) needs Newtek’s NDI runtime built into the app, which is a separate step.

Until then you can already use NDI with free NDI Tools:

• Receive: NDI Tools “Virtual Input” appears in the Camera list (Camera / Capture card). Choose the NDI source in its tray menu.

• Send: open the Output window, make it full screen, then use NDI Tools “Screen Capture” on it.

Settings

Paste the server URL and stream key from each platform’s live-streaming page. Twitch shows a list of nearby servers to choose from. Use the eye button to show or hide a key. Keys are saved on this PC only.

Your internet upload speed should be at least 1.5 times the bitrate, multiplied by the number of destinations you turn on.

Click Set, then press the key (or key combination) you want. Shortcuts work whenever you are not typing in a box.

720p is recommended for most computers and internet connections.
